## Zone Quick Reference — Best Gene Farming Per Region

### Azuria (Early)
- **Fire genes**: Rathalos, Rathian, Anjanath
- **Thunder genes**: Lagiacrus, Astalos, Tobi-Kadachi
- **Water genes**: Mizutsune, Royal Ludroth
- **Poison/Status**: Dreadqueen Rathian, Purple Gypceros
- **Notable**: Dancer (XL) from Mizutsune, Solar Cry from Dreadqueen/Boltreaver

### Canalta (Mid)
- **Critical Eye**: Nargacuga, Zinogre (both S-tier)
- **Dragon genes**: Deviljho, Gore Magala
- **Thunder genes**: Zinogre, Ivory Lagiacrus
- **Ice genes**: Legiana, Aurora Somnacanth, Gammoth
- **Notable**: Partner (XL) from Zinogre, Devour from Deviljho

### Tarkuan (Mid-Late)
- **Non-Elem offense**: Tigrex, Diablos
- **HtH Master**: Tigrex, Grimclaw Tigrex
- **Fire genes**: Glavenus, Espinas, Uragaan
- **Thunder genes**: Thunderlord Zinogre, Stygian Zinogre (Dragon)
- **Notable**: All-Out (XL) from Brachydios

### Serathis (Late)
- **Dragon genes**: Magnamalo, Malzeno
- **Ice genes**: Barioth, Lunagaron
- **Sustain**: Malzeno (Bloodblight, Life Drain, Crit-Heal)
- **Notable**: Bloodblight (XL) from Malzeno is top sustain gene

### Post-game (Elder Dragons)
- **Best-in-slot passives**: Nergigante (Self-Heal, Tenacity), Velkhana (Panacea, Skill Seal)
- **Dragon offense**: Ruiner Nergigante, Nergigante
- **Fire offense**: Teostra (Supernova)
- **Ice control**: Velkhana, Kushala Daora
- **Water**: Namielle
- **Thunder**: Kirin
- **Notable**: Elder Dragon genes are often exclusive — no earlier alternatives
