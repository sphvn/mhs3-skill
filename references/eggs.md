# MHS3 Eggs Reference — Patterns, Potency & Dens

## How to Get Monsties
1. Explore overworld → enter monster dens (grey/gold/platinum rock entrances)
2. Steal eggs from nests inside dens
3. Hatch eggs at a stable
4. Each egg has a visual pattern hinting at the species inside

## Color Variations
Within each genus pattern, **color tint indicates the specific species**:
- Red-tinted zebra stripes → Rathalos
- Blue-tinted zebra stripes → Azure Rathalos or Legiana
- After your first hatch of a species, the game shows the exact monster name on future eggs of that species

## Deviant Egg Patterns
Deviant monsties (Dreadqueen Rathian, Boltreaver Astalos, Silverwind Nargacuga, etc.) share the **same base genus pattern** as their normal counterpart. Key differences:
- Sharper color contrasts or unique accent colors
- Found in: high-rank dens, retreat dens after defeating the deviant

## Potency Levels
| Potency | Visual Cue | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Fairly Potent | Dim glow | Fewer unlocked gene slots, lower gene quality |
| Very Potent | Moderate glow | More gene slots, better default genes |
| Highly Potent | Rainbow glow | Most gene slots unlocked, highest quality default genes |

**Important**: Potency only affects the initial gene pool of the hatched monstie. It does NOT affect base stats. A Fairly Potent Rathalos has the same base stats as a Highly Potent one — the genes can always be replaced via Rite of Channeling.

**Practical advice**: Don't waste time farming Highly Potent eggs if you plan to replace all genes anyway. Potency matters most when you want good starter genes or plan minimal channeling.

## Den Types
| Den Type | Entrance | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| Normal (grey rock) | Common, everywhere | Standard eggs, common monsties |
| Gold (gold rock) | Rarer | Better odds for rare species, higher potency |
| Platinum (platinum rock) | Rarest | Best odds for rare species, highest potency |
| Retreat den | After defeating monster | Monster-specific egg guaranteed (paintball trick) |

## Paintball Egg Farming Trick
To reliably get 2 Highly Potent eggs from a specific species:
1. Use a **Paintball** on the monster before defeating it (even via Quick Finish)
2. Monster flees to its own nest
3. Gather 2 eggs from the nest
4. Optionally defeat the monster again in the nest to skip Rudy's dialogue when picking eggs

## Habitat Restoration
- Rebuild monster habitats in regions to unlock new dens
- Can introduce monsters to areas where they don't naturally spawn
- Completing restoration projects can grant access to otherwise unavailable eggs
- Key for deviant monsties and rare variants
- **Note**: Monsters introduced via Habitat Restoration do NOT have eggs in those areas — only their native region eggs count

## Retreat Dens (Green Cave)
When a monster is defeated in the overworld, it may retreat to a **Green Cave** den. This den always contains eggs of that specific species — the most reliable farming method for targeted monsties.

## SR Expedition Tickets (Post-game Elder Dragon farming)
- Cost: **100 Bottle Caps** each at the Bottle Cap Trader
- Bottle Caps earned from: subquests, achievements, multiplayer
- SR tickets guarantee access to high-rank dens where Elder Dragon eggs can spawn
- Elder Dragons only appear at night — rest at camp to change time
- A single SR run can yield multiple eggs — prioritize Highly Potent (rainbow glow)

## Day/Night Cycle
- Different monsters appear at day vs night
- Some monsties are night-exclusive (notably Elder Dragons in post-game)
- Rest at camp or use items to change time of day
- Check whether your target monstie spawns during day or night before farming

## Egg Quality Tip
When picking up eggs, **smell = gene quality**. "Smells amazing" or similar positive comments indicate better default genes and higher potency. Prioritize these when farming.
