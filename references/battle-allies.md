# MHS3 Battle Allies Reference — All 6 Partners

You bring **one partner per quest**. Each has a fixed weapon, element, attack type, and companion monstie.

## Change Battle Allies Screen
- Select allies to join you in battle
- View ranger and monstie abilities from this screen
- Change ranger equipment and monsties
- **New ranger equipment and monsties are unlocked by completing Side Stories** — not from crafting or the main quest line

## Partner Details

### Ogden (S Tier) — Best Overall
- **Role**: Buffer/Controller via Hunting Horn
- **Strengths**: Attack/defense buffs for entire party, trap setups that cripple bosses, Wyvernfell burst damage, works in every team composition
- **Weaknesses**: Lower personal DPS, Chirpy (Tech/Normal) has average coverage
- **When to pick**: Default choice. Every team benefits from Ogden.

### Kora (A Tier) — Tank
- **Role**: Damage soak, aggro redirect
- **Strengths**: Highest HP and defense of all partners, redirects attacks to herself, Gravy adds Fire/Power coverage, essential for hard content
- **Weaknesses**: Low damage output, Fire element overlaps with Eleanor
- **When to pick**: Hard boss fights, survival-priority content

### Simon (A Tier) — Early Game DPS
- **Role**: Fast Thunder damage dealer
- **Strengths**: Strong evasion skills, available from Act 1, Fawn fills Ice/Speed gap early monsties often miss
- **Weaknesses**: Falls off in endgame vs specialized picks, Thunder coverage shared with Thea
- **When to pick**: Acts 1–2, when Thunder coverage matters

### Thea (B Tier) — Status Specialist
- **Role**: Ranged status applicator
- **Strengths**: Poison/Paralysis debuffs, reduces enemy damage, ranged (avoids melee AoE), Kagachi adds Thunder/Speed synergy
- **Weaknesses**: Lower direct damage than Simon, Thunder overlap, status less useful on high-resist bosses
- **When to pick**: Fights where status effects stick and Poison/Paralysis payoff is high

### Eleanor (B Tier) — Glass Cannon
- **Role**: Burst Power damage
- **Strengths**: Highest burst damage of all partners, Great Sword dominates Head-to-Heads, Angie doubles Fire/Power, excellent for speed clears
- **Weaknesses**: Fragile (needs tank support), Fire useless vs fire-resistant foes, late unlock
- **When to pick**: Speed clears, pair with Ogden or Kora for protection

### Gaul (C Tier) — Sustain/Healer
- **Role**: Party sustain, item conservation
- **Strengths**: Best self-sustain of all partners, Ice covers Dragon-weak targets, Legia adds Ice/Speed, keeps party topped off on long expeditions
- **Weaknesses**: Lowest damage ceiling, healing less valuable than Kora's prevention or Ogden's buffs
- **When to pick**: Marathon exploration runs where item conservation matters

## Recommended Partner by Scenario

| Scenario | Best Pick | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Story progression | Ogden | Buffs smooth out any encounter |
| Early game (Acts 1–2) | Simon | Available early, Thunder + Ice coverage |
| Hard boss fights | Kora or Ogden | Survival + buffs |
| Speed clears | Eleanor | Highest burst, pair with offensive monsties |
| Status-heavy fights | Thea | Debuffs reduce boss effectiveness |
| Long expeditions | Gaul | Conserves items, party sustain |
