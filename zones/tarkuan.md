# Tarkuan — Progression Guide (Mid-Late Game)

Volcanic deserts and rocky terrain. Ranks 4–6. 19 monsties available.

## Priority Monsties

| Monstie | Type | Element | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Espinas | Power | Fire | Noxious Poison for continuous damage. Bountiful Dunes or secret retreat in Azuria |
| Brute Tigrex | Power | Non-Elem | Tigrex Rank A any habitat. Raw damage powerhouse |
| Stygian Zinogre | Tech | Dragon | Zinogre Rank A Habitat Restoration. Dragon/Tech coverage |
| Thunderlord Zinogre | Power | Thunder | Rank 7 deviant. Thunder Boost (XL), Critical Eye+ |
| Glavenus | Power | Fire | Solid Fire option with strong actives |

**Carry forward**: Dreadqueen Rathian (S-tier), Boltreaver Astalos (S-tier), Deviljho, Zinogre — all still top picks

## Gear Progression

**Weapons** (Ta zone and earlier):
- Great Sword: Scorching Blazer (Fire, Charge Burst L) or Donnerzahn (Thunder, GS Mastery L)
- Long Sword: Usurper's Boltslicer (Thunder, LS Mastery L) or Dios Katana (Fire, Brachydios)
- Hammer: Diablos Hammer (Non-Elem, Striking Secret L) or Scorching Isshata (Fire, Mastery L)
- Hunting Horn: Rey Perun (Thunder, Polychrome L) or Golm Drum
- Bow: Still Crystalline Flower (Ca) or Blue Blade Bow — Bow options limited until Se/PG
- Gunlance: Still Aknosom/Almudron from Ca — GL options limited here

**Armor**: Tigrex Armor (HtH Master) → Diablos Armor (Potential L) → Glavenus Armor (Elemental Breaker L + Assault L, 3 deco). Espinas Armor is great for status builds.

## Gene Farming Plan

- **HtH Master (XL)**: Tigrex, Grimclaw Tigrex — top Kinship-on-H2H-win passive
- **Non-Elem Atk Boost**: Tigrex, Diablos — for raw damage builds
- **All-Out (XL)**: Brachydios — active skill damage +40%
- **Fire genes**: Glavenus, Espinas, Uragaan — stronger fire options than Azuria
- **Thunder genes**: Thunderlord Zinogre — highest-tier Thunder genes available before PG
- **Dragon**: Stygian Zinogre supplements Deviljho from Canalta
- Tarkuan environment skills boost Kinship Gauge and Riding Gauge — ideal for Crit-Kinship engine builds

## Build Recommendation

**Team**: Espinas (P/Fire) + Stygian Zinogre (T/Dragon) + Brute Tigrex (P/Non-Elem) or carry Dreadqueen (S/Fire)
- All four can apply debuffs that stall enemies
- Espinas Noxious Poison = continuous damage, top-tier pick
- Swap in Boltreaver Astalos (T/Thunder) for type triangle coverage

**Battle Ally**: Ogden remains default. Kora for hard Tarkuan bosses. Eleanor if you want speed clears (she unlocks late).

## Tips

- Many Tarkuan monsters favor Power attacks when enraged — bring Speed counters
- Bloodbath Diablos (Rank 7 deviant) is here — one of the strongest Non-Elem monsties if you can get it
- Start planning Excursion stat bonuses now — Tarkuan environment skills (Kinship/Riding Gauge) are among the best
- Late-story bosses are often weak to Dragon — keep Deviljho leveled
