# Serathis — Progression Guide (Late Game)

Frozen peaks and crystal caverns. Ranks 5–7. 11 monsties available.

## Priority Monsties

| Monstie | Type | Element | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Malzeno | Tech | Dragon | A-tier. Bloodblight (XL) + Life Drain = self-sustaining DPS. Best Technical/Dragon |
| Magnamalo | Power | Dragon | Blastblight burst damage. Strong raw Dragon attacker |
| Rajang | Power | Thunder | A-tier. Raw Thunder powerhouse. High risk/reward |
| Barioth | Speed | Ice | Evasion specialist. Tenacity passive |
| Lunagaron | Speed | Ice | Ice/Speed option. Eggs in Old Capital Road |
| Rakna-Kadaki | Tech | Fire | Status specialist (Burning Silk). Fire/Tech niche |

**Carry forward**: Dreadqueen Rathian, Boltreaver Astalos, Deviljho — these S/A-tier monsties remain your backbone

## Gear Progression

**Weapons** (Se zone and earlier):
- Great Sword: Sword of the Winter Moon (Ice, Charge Burst L) or Sinister Blade (3 deco)
- Long Sword: Khanga Edge (Non-Elem, LS Mastery L, 0 deco but skill-dense) or Glacial Shotel (Ice)
- Hammer: Hammer of the Winter Moon (Ice, Soulbreak L) or Sinister Hammer (3 deco)
- Hunting Horn: Glacial Bagpipe (Ice, Mending Musician L) or Sinister Strum (3 deco)
- Bow: Iceflinger (Ice, Deft Hands L) or Weaver of Flame (Fire, Rakna-Kadaki)
- Gunlance: Still limited — carry Ca options or wait for PG

**Armor**: Barioth Armor (Crit L + Sealbreaker L) → Lunagaron Armor (Evasion + Vigilance) → Malzeno Armor (Self-Heal XL, best sustain armor). Damascus Armor is excellent balanced defense. Sinister Armor (HtH Master XL) for Head-to-Head specialists.

## Gene Farming Plan

- **Bloodblight (XL)**: Malzeno — top sustain gene, Technical/Dragon
- **Life Drain**: Malzeno — HP recovery on attacks
- **Crit-Heal (XL)**: Malzeno — heal 30% HP on crit
- **Dragon genes**: Magnamalo, Malzeno — strongest Dragon genes before PG
- **Ice genes**: Barioth, Lunagaron — solid ice sources
- **Evasion**: Malzeno, Rajang — upgraded Evasion passives
- Serathis environment skills trigger when losing a Heart (HP threshold) — synergizes with Heroics/Vigilance builds

## Build Recommendation

**Team**: Deviljho (P/Dragon) + Boltreaver Astalos (T/Thunder) + Malzeno (T/Dragon) or Lunagaron (S/Ice)
- Late-story bosses are often Dragon-weak — Deviljho essential
- Magnamalo Blastblight provides burst windows
- Malzeno sustains through long boss fights

**Battle Ally**: Ogden or Kora. Serathis bosses hit hard — Kora's damage soak may be more valuable than Ogden's buffs here.

## Tips

- Maximize attack and defense for endgame — gene optimization matters more than ever
- Many Serathis monsters are Ice-element — bring Fire coverage
- Start sending monsties on Excursions to max Ecosystem Rank before post-game
- You're close to the end of the story — plan your Elder Dragon farming team now
