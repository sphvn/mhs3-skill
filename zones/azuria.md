# Azuria — Progression Guide (Early Game)

Grasslands and coasts. Starting region. Ranks 1–5. 28 monsties available.

## Priority Monsties

| Monstie | Type | Element | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dreadqueen Rathian | Speed | Fire | S-tier. Highest HP, AoE poison+burn, Solar Cry (+30 Kinship). Get via Rathian Ecosystem Rank S + 3 Poison-skill monsties after Endangered Species quest |
| Boltreaver Astalos | Tech | Thunder | S-tier. Burst damage king, Solar Cry. Get via Astalos Ecosystem Rank A + 3 Thunder monsties |
| Purple Gypceros | Power | Non-Elem | A-tier. Multi-status disruptor. Immediately available in Broadleaf Basin |
| Rathalos | Power | Fire | Your starter Ratha evolves. Solid all-rounder |
| Mizutsune | Tech | Water | Best Water option for a while. Dancer (XL) passive |

**Early fillers** (replace as better options appear): Velocidrome, Arzuros, Yian Kut-Ku, Pukei-Pukei

## Gear Progression

**Weapons** (Shop + Az zone):
- Great Sword: Iron Sword (Shop) → Flammenzahn (Fire) or Lagiacrus Blade (Thunder)
- Long Sword: Wyvern Blade "Verde" (Fire/Rathian) or Spark Slicer (Thunder)
- Hammer: Kut-Ku Pick (Fire) or Ludroth Splashhammer (Water)
- Hunting Horn: Frog Flute (Water) or Kut-Ku's Song (Fire)
- Bow: Kut-Ku Stave (Fire) or Kadachi Strikebow (Thunder)
- Gunlance: Barroth Loader or Lagia Burst (Thunder)

**Armor**: Rathian Armor → Kadachi Armor → Rath Heart Armor → Rathalos Armor (best Az armor)

## Gene Farming Plan

- **Fire genes**: Rathalos, Rathian, Anjanath — abundant here
- **Thunder genes**: Lagiacrus, Astalos, Tobi-Kadachi
- **Water genes**: Mizutsune, Royal Ludroth
- **Poison/Status**: Dreadqueen Rathian (Noxious Poison, Inflict Rate Up)
- **Dancer (XL)**: Mizutsune — excellent early passive
- **Solar Cry**: Dreadqueen or Boltreaver — Kinship +30, crucial for hard fights
- Critical Eye available but only S-tier here; better sources exist later

## Build Recommendation

**Team**: Dreadqueen Rathian (S/Fire) + Boltreaver Astalos (T/Thunder) + Purple Gypceros (P/Non-Elem)
- Covers all three attack types (S/T/P)
- Solar Cry on Dreadqueen + Boltreaver = fast Kinship access
- Purple Gypceros provides crowd control

**Battle Ally**: Simon (Thunder/Speed, available from Act 1) — fills the Ice/Speed gap your early monsties lack. Switch to Ogden once available.

## Tips

- Rest at camp to swap day/night — some monsties are night-exclusive
- Turn off **Auto-Centering** (Options > Camera) for better exploration control
- Hold A to gather items in the field instead of tapping
- The **caution icon** (dragon above monsters) means they're much higher level — avoid or disable in Options > Game Settings
- Recipes for potions/traps must be purchased from vendors — check the Combining Menu
