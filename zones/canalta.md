# Canalta Timberland — Progression Guide (Mid Game)

Mountains and timberlands. Ranks 3–6. 24 monsties available.

## Priority Monsties

| Monstie | Type | Element | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deviljho | Power | Dragon | A-tier. Early Dragon powerhouse, scales into endgame. Wyvernsoul Defense Down. Northeast of Cataracts Camp |
| Deadeye Yian Garuga | Speed | Fire | Wyvernsoul Defense Down. Yian Garuga Rank S + 3 five-star monsties |
| Zinogre | Power | Thunder | A-tier. Charged state powerhouse. Critical Eye + Partner (XL) passives |
| Nargacuga | Speed | Non-Elem | Critical Eye source. Evasion specialist |
| Aurora Somnacanth | Tech | Ice | Fills Technical/Ice gap. Eggs in Mt. Canalta |
| Gore Magala | Power | Dragon | Dragon alternative to Deviljho |

**Carry forward from Azuria**: Dreadqueen Rathian, Boltreaver Astalos, Rathalos — all still excellent

## Gear Progression

**Weapons** (Ca zone and earlier):
- Great Sword: Gossblade (Ice) or Ceanataur Blade (Water) or Epitaph Blade (Dragon)
- Long Sword: Daito Crow (Non-Elem, Nargacuga) or Reaver "Cruelty" (Dragon, Deviljho)
- Hammer: Ceanataur Head Axe (Water) or Frozen Core (Thunder)
- Hunting Horn: Hydros Horn (Water, best support HH) or Raven Shamisen
- Bow: Blue Blade Bow (Water) or Crystalline Flower (Ice)
- Gunlance: Aknosom Gunlance (Fire) or Almudron Gunlance (Water)

**Armor**: Nargacuga Armor (Crit + Evasion) → Zinogre Armor (Partner L) → Legiana Armor (Ice Atk + Quick). Rath Soul Armor is craftable here and is the best DPS armor in the game.

## Gene Farming Plan

- **Critical Eye**: Nargacuga and Zinogre — both here, farm these
- **Dragon genes**: Deviljho, Gore Magala
- **Thunder genes**: Zinogre (stronger than Azuria sources)
- **Ice genes**: Legiana, Aurora Somnacanth, Gammoth
- **Partner (XL)**: Zinogre — massive Kinship generation under 50% HP
- **Devour**: Deviljho — HP steal active skill, great for sustain builds
- Better versions of Fire/Water genes exist later — don't over-invest here

## Build Recommendation

**Team**: Deviljho (P/Dragon) + Deadeye Yian Garuga (S/Fire) + Aurora Somnacanth (T/Ice)
- Full type triangle coverage
- Deviljho + Deadeye both apply Wyvernsoul Defense Down for faster knockdowns
- Covers Dragon, Fire, Ice damage

**Battle Ally**: Ogden (Tech/HH) — buffs smooth out all encounters. Kora if you need survivability.

## Tips

- Deviljho eggs are in dens northeast of Cataracts Camp — force retreat for best odds
- Green Nargacuga available via Nargacuga Rank A Habitat Restoration — slightly different gene pool
- Canalta environment skills focus on self-buff duration and Head-to-Head bonuses — good for Crit-Kinship builds
- Start thinking about Bingo optimization now — you have enough gene variety to build proper 3-line setups
