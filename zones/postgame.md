# Post-game — Progression Guide (Elder Dragons & Endgame)

Endgame content unlocked after clearing the main story. 8 Elder Dragons. All regions accessible.

## Elder Dragon Unlock Flow

1. Complete main story and defeat final boss
2. Unlocks High Rank expeditions across all regions
3. Elder Dragons spawn at **night only** in random regions
4. Use **SR Expedition Tickets** (100 Bottle Caps each from Bottle Cap Trader) for guaranteed high-rank dens
5. Elder Dragon eggs have **star pattern** — look for them in SR dens at night

## Elder Dragons — All 8

| Monstie | Type | Element | Signature |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nergigante | Power | Dragon | Self-healing spikes — HP regen as it deals damage |
| Ruiner Nergigante | Power | Dragon | Enhanced self-heal + strongest raw physical damage |
| Velkhana | Tech | Ice | **Skill Seal** — disables opponent skills. PvP dominant |
| Teostra | Power | Fire | Supernova — devastating Fire AoE |
| Kushala Daora | Speed | Ice | Wind disruption — battlefield control |
| Namielle | Tech | Water | Water/lightning hybrid AoE — unique dual-element |

| Yama Tsukami | Tech | Dragon | Environmental control — unique battlefield manipulation |

## Endgame Gear

**Weapons** (PG tier — best in game):
- Great Sword: Veldian Gladius (Dragon, 3 deco) or Azure Elder GS
- Long Sword: Veldian Ensis (Dragon, 3 deco + LS Mastery L) or Reaver "Cruelty" (Dragon, 5 skills)
- Hammer: Azure Elder Hammer (Mastery L, 5 skills) or Veldian Malleus (3 deco)
- Hunting Horn: Duke's Grail (Dragon, Mending Musician L) or Veldian Sibilus (HH Mastery L, 3 deco)
- Bow: Veldian Arcum (Dragon, Deft Hands L, 3 deco) — best bow in the game
- Gunlance: Fiore Nulo Black (Dragon, 3 deco) — limited PG GL options

**Armor**: Rimeguard Armor (Velkhana — Kinship Skill+ XL, Panacea L, Divine Blessing L) is the best endgame survival set. Rath Soul Armor remains best for pure DPS. Arkvulcan Armor (Teostra — Synchronize XL) for Kinship builds.

## Gene Farming Plan — Best-in-Slot

- **Calamity Slash (XL)**: Nergigante — massive Power/Dragon single-hit
- **Skill Seal (XL)**: Velkhana — locks opponent skills, PvP essential
- **Supernova**: Teostra — devastating Fire AoE
- **Self-Heal**: Nergigante — HP regen passive
- **Panacea (XL)**: Velkhana — status immunity
- **Elder Seal**: Teostra, Ruiner Nergigante — anti-Elder Dragon
- **Tenacity**: Nergigante — survive one fatal hit
- Elder Dragon genes are often **exclusive** — no earlier alternatives exist

## Endgame Team Compositions

- **Balanced PvE**: Dreadqueen Rathian (S/Fire) + Boltreaver Astalos (T/Thunder) + Ruiner Nergigante (P/Dragon)
- **Elemental coverage**: Add Velkhana (Ice) or Namielle (Water) to the above
- **PvP**: Velkhana (Skill Seal) + burst damage (Boltreaver/Rajang) + status (Dreadqueen)
- **Crit-Kinship engine**: Ruiner Nergigante with Critical Eye XL + Soul Kinship XL + Critical Kinship XL

## Tips

- Elder Dragons are **night-only** — rest at camp to change time of day
- SR Expedition Tickets are 100 Bottle Caps each — farm Bottle Caps from high-rank quest rewards
- Highly Potent Elder Dragon eggs give better odds for XL-tier genes
- Focus Excursions on S-ranking all 4 environments for your core team — unlocks dual element + environment skills
- PvP Network Battles use separate matchmaking — build a dedicated PvP team (Velkhana + status pressure)
