# Eggs — MHS3

Eggs hatch into monsties. Each egg's visual pattern hints at the species genus.

## Getting Monsties

1. Explore overworld → enter dens (grey/gold/platinum rock entrances)
2. Steal eggs from nests inside dens
3. Hatch at a stable
4. Egg pattern + color = species hint

### Color Variations

Within a genus pattern, **color tint** indicates species:
- Red-tinted zebra → Rathalos
- Blue-tinted zebra → Azure Rathalos or Legiana
- After first hatch of a species, future eggs of that species show the exact name

### Deviant Egg Patterns

Deviant monsties (Dreadqueen, Boltreaver, Silverwind, etc.) share the **base genus pattern** with sharper color contrasts. Found in high-rank dens + retreat dens after defeating the deviant.

## Potency Levels

| Potency | Visual | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Fairly Potent | Dim glow | Fewer gene slots unlocked, lower gene quality |
| Very Potent | Moderate glow | More slots, better default genes |
| Highly Potent | Rainbow glow | Max slots, highest quality defaults |

**Potency only affects initial gene pool.** Base stats are identical regardless. If you plan to replace all genes via Rite of Channeling, potency doesn't matter.

## Den Types

| Type | Entrance | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| Normal | Grey rock, common | Standard eggs |
| Gold | Gold rock, rarer | Better odds for rare species, higher potency |
| Platinum | Platinum rock, rarest | Best odds rare + highest potency |
| Retreat den | After defeating monster | Monster-specific egg guaranteed (paintball trick) |
| Green cave | When monster retreats | Species-specific eggs — most reliable targeted farming |

## Paintball Egg Farming Trick

For 2 Highly Potent eggs of a target species:
1. Use **Paintball** before defeating the monster (even via Quick Finish)
2. Monster flees to its nest
3. Gather 2 eggs from the nest
4. Optionally defeat the monster in the nest to skip Rudy's dialogue

## Egg Smell Tip

When picking up eggs, **smell = gene quality**. "Smells amazing" indicates better default genes and higher potency. Prioritize these when farming.

## Habitat Restoration Note

Monsters introduced to zones via Habitat Restoration do NOT have eggs in those areas — only native region eggs count. See [../habitats/habitats.md](../habitats/habitats.md) for ecosystem mechanics.

### Post-game (Elder Dragons — night-only)
All 8 Elder Dragons spawn randomly at night across all four regions. Requires main-story completion + High Rank access.

## SR Expedition Tickets (Post-game)

- Cost: **100 Bottle Caps** at the Bottle Cap Trader
- Bottle Caps earned from: subquests, achievements, multiplayer
- SR tickets guarantee access to high-rank dens where Elder Dragon eggs can spawn
- Elder Dragons only appear at night — rest at camp to change time
- A single SR run can yield multiple eggs — prioritize Highly Potent (rainbow)

## Day/Night Cycle

- Different monsters day vs night
- Some monsties are night-exclusive (notably Elder Dragons post-game)
- Rest at camp or use items to change time
- Check target monstie's spawn time before farming
