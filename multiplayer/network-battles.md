# Network Battles (PvP) — MHS3

MHS3 has **PvP Network Battles only** — there is NO co-op multiplayer. The main story is entirely single-player.

## What Network Battles Are

- **3v3** turn-based PvP using your Rider + 3 monsties vs another player's team.
- Matchmaking is elo-based (approximate skill rating).
- You bring a roster of monsties and the match picks active team from your available 3.
- All your gene/armor/weapon/excursion work transfers — no PvP-specific progression.

## What Network Battles Are Not

- **No co-op** against the AI. You can't team up to fight a boss together.
- No async "ghost" battles with other players' teams.
- No ranked ladders with cosmetic rewards (check game updates — as of launch there's a basic ranking system only).

## PvP Meta (Launch Window)

**⚠️ SEARCH FLAG**: The PvP meta shifts with balance patches. For current tier lists, search recent posts on r/MonsterHunterStories or Game8's PvP tier list page.

### Top PvP Monsties (as of launch)

| Monstie | Why |
|---|---|
| **Velkhana** | Skill Seal locks the opponent out of their skills — AI can't counter; players often can't either if caught with key cooldowns |
| **Silverwind Nargacuga** | 20% base crit + White Shadow (2-turn full evasion) — extreme dodge windows |
| **Espinas** | Dual status (Paralysis + Noxious Poison) applies pressure before they can respond |
| **Magnamalo** | Blastblight detonation on-demand — can one-shot low-HP enemies |
| **Ruiner Nergigante** | Self-heal tank; hard to burst down |

### PvP Build Principles (differs from PvE)

1. **Skills matter more than stats**. A Skill Seal monstie shuts down your opponent regardless of their stats.
2. **Evasion > HP**. Dodge windows (White Shadow, Dancer, Evasion Ability) matter more in PvP than tanky HP, since opponents adapt.
3. **Status applies faster** than in PvE. Inflict Rate Up + status genes can cripple a match in 2 turns.
4. **Stamina economy is everything**. See [../genes/builds.md](../genes/builds.md) — higher Stamina Regen lets you pressure consistently.
5. **Burst > Sustain**. PvP fights are short. Bloodbath Diablos glass-cannon builds win before their own HP becomes an issue.

### PvP Counter-Picks

| Their Pick | Counter |
|---|---|
| Velkhana (Skill Seal) | Sealbreaker armor passive OR Zest Pill items + fast burst before Seal lands |
| Silverwind Nargacuga (evasion) | Accuracy Up from meals, Accuracy-ignoring Wyvernsoul damage |
| Espinas (dual status) | Panacea (XL) passive, Antivenom + Antiparalysis stack |
| Magnamalo (Blastblight) | Blightproof armor passive, kill monstie before blast triggers |
| Tank (Ruiner Nergigante / Dreadqueen) | Wyvernsoul Topple team — ignore their regen, break Stocks, toppel guaranteed-crit |

## Match Flow

1. **Pre-match**: you pick 3 monsties from your roster.
2. **Match**: turn-based, identical to PvE except opponent is human-controlled.
3. **Item usage**: limited — you get a restricted stack of potions/traps.
4. **Result**: win/loss affects matchmaking rating.

## Items You Can Bring

Limited pool — typically healing potions, status cures, and a small trap allotment. No paintball or SR tickets.

## Preparation

1. Build 3 monsties optimized for PvP (status + evasion + burst).
2. Armor: balance HP and utility — pure tank or pure DPS loses to balanced.
3. Weapon: match your Rider's attack type to your top monstie for H2H synergy.
4. Partner: **Ogden** (Hunting Horn) usually best — party buffs matter.

## Strategic Tips

- **Attack first with fast monsties**. Speed trumps in opening turns.
- **Predict H2H**. If opponent's monstie favors Power, lead with Technical.
- **Save Kinship for critical moments**, not immediate use.
- **Preserve stamina**. Don't dump all skills in turn 1.
